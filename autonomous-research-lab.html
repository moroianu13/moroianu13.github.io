Autonomous Research Lab

A domain-agnostic local multi-agent scientific research platform designed to investigate testable questions across fields by discovering evidence, acquiring structured data, challenging hypotheses, performing analysis and producing provenance-backed conclusions.

StatusActive development
ScopeDomain-agnostic research
RuntimeLocal model orchestration
Core principleEvidence before conclusions
Scientific flow

Research as an auditable pipeline.

The lab is designed around a scientific sequence rather than an open-ended group chat. Literature provides context; original claims must come from executed, provenance-backed evidence.

Question→Existing knowledge→Research gap→Data / experiments→Statistics→Replication→Judge

The platform is intentionally not tied to economics or any single provider. Research sources and APIs are treated as pluggable inputs to a broader scientific workflow.

Grounded evidenceSource-aware retrieval and structured evidence artifacts.
Counter-evidenceDedicated challenge path for falsification and strongest-opposition search.
Statistical analysisDeterministic analysis paths and explicit inference boundaries.
ProvenancePersisted artifacts, lineage and auditable research-cycle state.
Fail-closed judgmentUnresolved questions remain unresolved when evidence is insufficient.
Multi-source integrationResearch APIs, literature and structured datasets can be added as bounded sources.
Agent orchestration

Specialized roles, visible handoffs.

The Research Director coordinates the workflow while specialist roles gather evidence, challenge it, analyze data, synthesize findings and make the final scientific judgment.

Research Director→Researcher↔Counter-Researcher→Skeptic→Data Scientist→Evidence Synthesizer→Judge
Engineering quality

Built as software, not a prompt demo.

The current documented baseline includes 500+ automated tests, static checks and typed Python modules. The system also has real source-backed research runs and preserves negative or unresolved results rather than forcing a conclusion.
