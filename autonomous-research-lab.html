Autonomous Research Lab

A domain-agnostic multi-agent scientific research platform for testable questions, evidence, analysis and provenance.

← Projects

In short

The lab coordinates specialised agents through an explicit research pipeline instead of treating multi-agent chat as the final product.

Stack / themes

PythonMulti-agent systemsResearch automationStatisticsData provenanceLocal AI

Research workflow

A research question moves through evidence gathering, counter-evidence, synthesis, statistical or empirical analysis where appropriate, sceptical review and a final judge. The goal is to make the process observable and auditable.

Domain agnostic

The lab is designed to work across domains such as medicine, economics, agriculture, technology or public policy when suitable evidence and data are available.
