Autonomous Research Lab

A domain-agnostic local multi-agent scientific research platform designed to take testable questions through evidence gathering, counter-evidence, analysis, synthesis and final scientific judgment.

Live research environment

The agents have roles, handoffs and persisted work.

The interface is an observability layer over a real research cycle. It shows the research office, current agent state, handoffs and live engine events without replacing the underlying evidence pipeline.

Research pipeline

Evidence-driven and falsification-aware by design.

The lab is not tied to economics, medicine or any single provider. It is designed around the research method: formalize the question, discover relevant evidence, find the research gap, acquire appropriate data, run analysis, challenge the result and only then synthesize a conclusion.

Question→Knowledge→Gap→Evidence / data→Analysis→Counter-evidence→Judge
What I builtA coordinated multi-agent research runtime, artifact pipeline, provenance-aware evidence flow and a live control interface for inspecting a research cycle as it executes.
What makes it differentThe project is built around research discipline rather than agent conversation: evidence, uncertainty, counter-arguments and persisted artifacts matter more than fluent output.

Domain-agnostic by design

One research engine, many possible fields.

The platform can work across medicine, economics, agriculture, environmental research, technology or public policy when the question is testable and the required evidence can be obtained. Individual data providers are integrations, not the identity of the lab.
