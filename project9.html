System overview

From job postings to repeatable market intelligence.

The project automates collection, normalization, classification and historical tracking of European data-science job postings, then exposes the results through an interactive dashboard.

Collect→Normalize→Classify→Track→Analyze→Dashboard

Automated pipelineScheduled ingestion and CI workflows.
