System overview

From raw postings to repeatable market intelligence.

The project automates collection, normalization, classification and historical tracking of European data-science job postings, then exposes the results through an interactive dashboard rather than leaving the analysis in notebooks.

Collect→Normalize→Classify→Track→Analyze→Dashboard
What I builtData ingestion and normalization workflows, ML role classification, historical job tracking, analytical transformations, tests and the user-facing dashboard.
Why it mattersThe project demonstrates the full path from messy external data to a repeatable analytical product that can be rerun and inspected over time.
ML role classificationTF-IDF + Logistic Regression for automated role labeling.
Historical trackingFingerprinting and snapshots for repost / ghost-job patterns.
Automated pipelineScheduled ingestion and CI workflows instead of manual one-off analysis.
Interactive analysisRole, salary, geography and skills exploration in a deployed UI.
