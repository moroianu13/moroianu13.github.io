NeuroAnatomy

An intelligent interactive 3D neuroanatomy atlas for academic and educational environments, designed to connect anatomical structures with function, arterial supply, venous drainage and clinically relevant learning context.

Product views

Anatomy becomes easier to understand when context stays attached to the structure.

The atlas combines an interactive 3D brain, searchable anatomical hierarchy and a structure-insight panel. A learner can locate a structure and immediately inspect its anatomy, function, vascular supply, drainage and educational clinical context.

Learning in context

Built for deeper understanding, not just anatomical viewing.

Selecting a structure connects 3D localization with its anatomical position, primary function, vascular context, clinical relevance and source-linked academic information.

Select structure→3D localization→Function→Vascular→Clinical context
What I builtThe product concept, information architecture and interactive educational experience that turns a 3D anatomical model into a structured academic exploration tool.
Product principleVisualization should answer “what is this, where is it, what does it do and how is it supplied?” without forcing the learner to jump between disconnected resources.

Educational / academic use. The atlas is not presented as a clinical diagnostic tool.
