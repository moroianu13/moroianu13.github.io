NeuroAnatomy

An intelligent interactive 3D neuroanatomy atlas built for academic and educational environments. It connects anatomical exploration with functional context, arterial supply, venous drainage and clinically relevant information.

StatusIn development
AudienceStudents · educators · medical professionals
PurposeLearning & academic exploration
PositioningIntelligent atlas, not just a viewer
Built for deeper understanding

Anatomy in context.

The goal is not simply to rotate a brain model. Selecting a structure should help the learner understand where it sits, what it does, how it is vascularized and why it matters.

Select structure→3D localization→Function→Arterial supply→Venous drainage→Clinical context

Educational / academic use. The atlas is not presented as a diagnostic or clinical decision-making tool.

Vascular exploration

Anatomy and blood supply in the same mental model.

The atlas can shift from structural exploration to vascular views, helping learners connect regions with arterial supply and venous drainage instead of studying them as separate diagrams.
