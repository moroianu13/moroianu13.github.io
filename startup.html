MedCore Systems

We are building secure medical AI infrastructure and clinical software for European healthcare — designed around hospitals, laboratories, healthcare technology partners and the realities of medical deployment.

Visit MedCore Systemsoffice@medcoresystems.com
Company direction

Infrastructure first, products on top.

MedCore is intentionally more than a software startup. The goal is to build the secure backend environment and the healthcare applications that can run on it, so infrastructure, governance, security and product design evolve together.

01

Secure medical AI infrastructure

Controlled private environments for healthcare workloads, with strong emphasis on privacy, integration, auditability and operational control.

02

Healthcare software products

Clinical and operational software built on the same stack, starting with NeuroPath AI and expanding toward additional healthcare workflows over time.

03

European cloud direction

Our long-term direction is a European healthcare cloud model supporting private hosted, on-premise and hybrid deployment rather than generic public SaaS assumptions.

Private medical AI infrastructure→Security, governance & integration→Healthcare software products
First product in development

NeuroPath AI

NeuroPath AI is MedCore's first medical AI product. It starts with decision-support workflows for acute stroke care, bringing clinical information, imaging inputs and structured guideline logic into one review path.

The public positioning is intentionally simple: it is built to support clinicians, not replace them. The product is still in development and validation planning before any production clinical use.

Long-term vision

A European healthcare technology stack.

The broader goal is to create a secure European environment where healthcare organisations can run medical AI and clinical software with stronger control over infrastructure, integration, governance and data location.

Today

Private infrastructure, controlled deployment environments, product development and partner integration for healthcare use cases.

Direction

A European healthcare cloud and shared platform where future MedCore products can reuse the same infrastructure, governance and deployment model.
